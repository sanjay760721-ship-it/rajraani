Inspired by the frescoes of ancient Roman villas, this saree translates their richly ornamented surfaces into the language of the Banarasi loom. Woven in Katan with textured gheecha silk, it pairs a deep chocolate-brown ground with panels of deep red, creating a contrast across the drape. The pallu and border are composed of framed panels, each centred around a symmetrical floral motif with curling forms extending from a central bloom. Small green accents define the motif, while the interplay of other colours gives the saree a depth reminiscent of aged frescoes, and the textured silk lends the entire composition a tactile quality.

Colour- An elegant Brown
Technique- An enduring Kadhua weave. This elaborate and laborious technique involves hand-weaving each motif separately. It takes longer on the handloom, but makes a more robust pattern which stands out on the fabric
Fabric- Pure Katan Silk
Speciality-Woven with Gheecha, a unique textured raw silk, the saree uses uneven, tactile yarns in extra-weft patterning to create an organic, earthy surface distinct from the polished richness of traditional zari brocades.
Tilfi Promise-Pure. Handloom. Banaras.
