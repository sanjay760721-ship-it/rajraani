ART & COLLECTIBLES
Preserving the timeless lexicon of Banarasi craftsmanship, tradition, and culture, Tilfi’s Art and Collectibles collection seeks to push the boundaries of design and artisanal excellence.
A celebration of India’s living artistry, reimagined to endure.


small photo


Tilfi draws from one of Banaras’s oldest and most revered craft traditions — the art of repoussé. Dating back to the Vedic era, the technique flourished long before the celebrated Banarasi handloom. Once used to adorn temples and ritual objects, it now finds renewed expression at Tilfi in creations that unite heritage, ingenuity, and function.
