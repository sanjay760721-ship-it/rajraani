# Changing the website — a guide for the shop

*Written 27 September 2026. For whoever runs the shop day to day. No technical
knowledge needed.*

Everything a visitor can read or see on the website can be changed from the admin:
words, photos, the menu, pages, pieces, prices, stock and orders. Nothing you do here
can break the design, because the design is fixed and only the content changes.

**Sign in** at `yourshop.com/admin`. The sidebar is grouped into *Overview*, *Your
website* and *Shop*. On a phone it is behind the **Menu** button at the top.

---

## The two fastest ways to change anything

### 1. Click it on the website

1. While signed in, open the shop itself.
2. A small dark bar sits at the bottom of the screen. Press **✏️ Edit this page**.
3. Point at anything: words or photos that can be changed get a gold dashed outline.
4. Click one. A panel opens on the right. Change the words, or upload or pick a photo.
5. Press **Save**. The page reloads showing your change, live.
6. Press **Done editing** when you have finished.

If the same words appear twice on a page, you will be asked which one you mean.
Clicking the menu takes you to the Menu screen instead.

### 2. Search for the words — *Change text*

1. Sidebar → **Change text**.
2. Type a few words exactly as they appear on the site, e.g. `Free shipping`.
3. Every place those words appear is listed, with where it is in plain words.
4. Change the words in the box and press **Save**. Each one goes live on its own.

Or pick a place on the left (e.g. *Our Banaras store*) to see all of its text at once.

> **After changing a fact** such as an email address, a phone number or a price,
> watch for the orange note: *"The old wording is still in 7 other places"*. Press
> **Show them** and change those too, so the site says the same thing everywhere.

## Pages, blocks and photos

Every page is a **stack of blocks**: a slideshow, a photo with text, a row of tiles,
questions and answers, and so on.

- **Homepage** and **Pages** show the blocks on the left, top to bottom. Pick one and
  its **Photos**, **Words**, **Button and link** and **How it looks** appear on the
  right.
- **+ Add a block** adds a new one after the one you picked. It starts as a copy of a
  real block, so you only change the words and photos.
- **Preview** shows the real page, on a computer or a phone.
- Nothing goes live until you press **Save & publish**. If you leave with unsaved
  changes, the admin asks first.

**Photos.**
- Upload straight from a camera or phone. They are resized automatically. Photos
  that are too small are refused, with a reason.
- Photos outlined in orange ("stand-in") are placeholders from the mock-up. They
  **must all be replaced before the shop goes public**. The **Photos** screen lists
  where they are.
- Upload all of a piece's photos in one sitting. Once a piece has any photo of your
  own, the stand-ins disappear from it.

**Links.** When you choose where a button or menu link goes, pick from the list. A
green ✓ confirms the page exists, and an orange ⚠ means no page has that address.

## Safety

- **Hide, don't delete.** Pieces and pages can be hidden from shoppers and shown
  again later. Deleting a piece is tucked away, because it cannot be undone.
- **New pages start hidden.** Tick **Live on the site** when a page is ready.
- **The admin refuses mistakes it can see**, and says what to fix: a link with no
  text, a menu with too many columns, a headline too long for its space.
- **Undo anything.** **Recent changes** lists every save with who made it. **Put back**
  returns that thing to how it was, and a put-back can itself be put back.
- **Two people editing the same page at once:** the last one to save wins. Agree who
  is editing what — and if it goes wrong, use Put back.

## Not possible yet

- seeing visitor numbers (a visitor-counting tool has to be chosen first)
- per-person permissions: everyone on the Team can change everything

**Online payment is not switched on yet.** The Orders, Overview and Discount codes
screens say so. Until it is, do not send anything for an order.
