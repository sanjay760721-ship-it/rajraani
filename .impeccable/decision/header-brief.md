# Surface: storefront header chrome (announcement strip + top bar)

Scope: `rajraani/src/components/AnnouncementBar.tsx`, `UtilityBar.tsx` and the shared utility styles. Mode: Persuade (it sits on every storefront page). Main menu row, mobile bar and mega menus are out of scope for this round.

Audience and job: brides with families, collectors and everyday-luxury buyers arriving at a royal Banarasi house; the strip states one true service promise, the bar gives search, currency, account, wishlist and cart.

Constraints: owner-editable words (announcement parts, house line); India-only shipping; 20px stacked icons over captions in a 64px bar are the owner's established preference; no reference-site traces; WCAG AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Arriving at Rajraani is stepping through a haveli door. Refuses the category default of a pastel or black utility strip with a tagline in italic serif.

OWN-WORLD: Lime-plaster white wall (#f6f3ee), indigo ink (#1f2433), sindoor lacquer (#7d1a1d) and hand-beaten brass (#a8894a) used only as metal: hairline rules and rings. Two inks only. Flat colour, no carving, pattern, gradient or texture.

STORY: The visitor reads one true promise on the lacquered lintel, sees the house named on a plaque, and finds the five actions where a door's studs would be.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 40px sindoor lintel, one message centred in lime-white small type, brass hairline at its lower edge. 64px lime-white wall: left, a brass-framed plaque holding the house line in indigo; right, five actions, each a 20px indigo icon over its caption; hover or focus draws a fine brass ring round the icon, nothing changes hue. Menu row below unchanged.

FORM: Haveli gateway, position 7 of the grounded list, seed key 56f06cd9. Raises: two-ink discipline (azulejo); one-course step motion for message change (depot blind); state as a mark, not a colour (cutting bench); fixed regions that never move (PC-98).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
